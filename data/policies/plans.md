# Mobile and home internet plans

## Mobile plans {#mobile}
Tessaline offers three mobile plans: Essential with 20 GB of data for $45 per month, Plus with 60 GB for $60 per month, and Unlimited with 150 GB at full speed for $80 per month. All mobile plans include unlimited Canada-wide calling and texting.

## Data after the monthly amount {#data-overage}
On Essential and Plus, data speed is reduced to 512 kbps after the monthly amount is used. There is no overage charge. On Unlimited, speed is reduced to 512 kbps after 150 GB.

## Home internet plans {#internet}
Home internet is available at 500 Mbps for $70 per month and 1 Gbps for $90 per month, both with unlimited usage. A modem is included at no charge and must be returned when service ends.

## Bundle discount {#bundle}
Customers with both a mobile plan and home internet receive a $10 monthly discount on the home internet plan.
