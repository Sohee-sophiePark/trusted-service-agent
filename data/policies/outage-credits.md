# Service outages and credits

## Outage updates {#updates}
Active outages are listed in the Tessaline app with the affected area, the start time, and the estimated restoration time. Estimates are updated at least every two hours.

## When a credit applies {#credit}
A service credit applies when home internet or mobile service is unavailable for more than 24 consecutive hours because of a network outage. The credit equals the daily cost of the plan for each full day without service.

## How credits are given {#how}
Outage credits are calculated after service is restored and appear on a following bill. Credits are applied automatically for confirmed network outages; customers do not need to request them.
