# Installations and repairs

## Appointment windows {#windows}
Installation and repair appointments are booked in four-hour windows: 8 am to 12 pm, 12 pm to 4 pm, or 4 pm to 8 pm.

## Ticket statuses {#statuses}
A ticket moves through these statuses: Received, Scheduled, Technician en route, In progress, Completed. A ticket can also be On hold when parts or access are needed.

## Status updates {#updates}
Technician updates reach customer records within one hour. If a ticket has not been updated for more than one hour, the latest status may not reflect the technician's progress.

## Repair timing {#timing}
Repair tickets for a full loss of service are scheduled within two business days. Other repair tickets are scheduled within five business days.
