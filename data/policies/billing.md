# Billing and payments

## Bill cycle {#cycle}
Each account has a monthly bill cycle that starts on the same day every month. A new bill is issued on the first day of the cycle and covers the next month of service in advance.

## Due date {#due-date}
Payment is due 21 days after the bill is issued. The due date is printed on the first page of every bill and shown in the Tessaline app.

## Payment methods {#methods}
Bills can be paid by pre-authorized debit, credit card, online banking, or in person at a Tessaline store. Online banking payments can take up to three business days to reach the account.

## Paper bills {#paper}
Paper bills are available on request at no charge for customers who are 65 or older or who receive a disability benefit. Other customers can receive a paper bill for $2 per month.
