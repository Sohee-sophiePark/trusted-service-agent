# Identity verification

## Before account details {#before}
Account details are shared only after the customer is verified. Verification needs the account number and the postal code of the service address.

## Authorized users {#authorized}
Only the account holder and authorized users listed on the account can receive account details. Authorized users are added by the account holder through a Tessaline agent.

## What is never asked {#never}
Tessaline never asks for a full password, a full card number, or a one-time code in chat.
