# Late payment charges

## When a late charge applies {#when}
A late payment charge applies when the full balance is not received by the due date. Partial payments reduce the balance but do not stop the charge.

## Amount {#amount}
The late payment charge is 2% of the overdue balance, with a minimum of $5 and a maximum of $25 per bill.

## Grace period {#grace}
No late charge applies if the payment is received within two business days after the due date.

## Payment arrangements {#arrangements}
Customers who cannot pay by the due date can ask a Tessaline agent about a payment arrangement before the due date. Arrangements are set up only by an agent.
