# Travel and roaming

## Travel Pass {#travel-pass}
Travel Pass lets customers use their Canadian plan in the United States for $12 per day and in other countries for $16 per day. A day starts when the phone first uses data, calls, or texts abroad.

## Daily cap {#cap}
Travel Pass charges stop after 10 days in the same bill cycle; further days in that cycle are included at no extra charge.

## Turning roaming off {#off}
Customers can turn roaming off in their phone settings to avoid Travel Pass charges. Wi-Fi calling and messaging over Wi-Fi do not trigger Travel Pass.
