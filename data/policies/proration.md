# Plan changes and prorated charges

## What proration means {#meaning}
When a plan or add-on changes part-way through a bill cycle, the bill shows a partial charge or credit for the days each plan was active. This is called a prorated charge.

## Why the next bill can be higher {#higher-bill}
After a mid-cycle change, the next bill can include both the prorated charge for the rest of the old cycle and the full monthly charge for the new cycle. The bill returns to the regular amount the cycle after.

## How proration is calculated {#calculation}
The prorated amount is the monthly price divided by the number of days in the cycle, multiplied by the number of days the plan was active.
